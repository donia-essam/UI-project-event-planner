About the night

The Cairo Quartet have been the house band at Zamalek Terrace every second Friday since spring. Expect a first set of standards, a short break while the sun finishes going down, then a late set of their own tunes, including a few from the album they’re still recording.

There’s a small bar on the roof and kofta sandwiches from the kitchen downstairs. Standing tickets get you anywhere along the back; tables sit right by the rail.
